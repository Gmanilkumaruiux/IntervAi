# IntervAI – AI Development Prompts

## Project
IntervAI – AI-Powered Mock Interview Platform

## AI Tools
- ChatGPT
- Antigravity

---

## Prompt 1 – Frontend

Build a modern AI interview platform using React, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS with authentication, dashboard, interview page, reports, learning roadmap and responsive design.

---

## Prompt 2 – Persistent Interview

Implement persistent interview sessions. Users should be able to leave the interview page and resume later without losing progress.

---

## Prompt 3 – Backend

Build a production-ready Flask backend using PostgreSQL, SQLAlchemy, JWT, Marshmallow and a modular architecture. Match the existing frontend without changing any UI.

---

## Prompt 4 – AI Interview

Generate dynamic interview questions based on role, skills, experience and previous answers. Avoid repeated questions and generate contextual follow-up questions.

---

## Prompt 5 – Reports

Generate interview reports including technical score, HR score, communication score, strengths, weaknesses, AI feedback and recommendations.

---

## Prompt 6 – Learning Roadmap

Generate a personalized learning roadmap based on interview performance, including weak skills, study topics, practice questions and learning resources.

---

## Prompt 7 – Voice Interview

Implement voice-only interview mode with Speech-to-Text, Text-to-Speech and transcript storage.

---

## Prompt 8 – Anti-Cheating

Detect tab switching, fullscreen exit, inactivity and copy/paste events. Log violations and include them in the interview report.

---

## Prompt 9 – Responsive Design

Make the application fully responsive for mobile, tablet and desktop without changing existing functionality.

---

## Prompt 10 – Deployment

Configure frontend and backend for local network access, production deployment and environment-based configuration.
